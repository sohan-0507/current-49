BOSSMODE FEATURES
- Project Charter is required and visible before joining.
- Charter has scope, rewards/payment terms, roles/eligibility, IP/publication, confidentiality/AI, credit split, exit/dispute, and future commercialisation clauses.
- Funded and Stipend / honorarium projects are paid and require age 18+ for participants.
- Knowledge-sharing and Institutional credit projects are explicitly non-monetary; joining requires a separate no-payment acknowledgement.
- Editing a project creates a new charter version and existing participants are marked Reaccept required.
- Participants can re-accept the new charter or leave with accepted contribution credit preserved in the ledger.
- Admin project management, sponsor marketplace, student applications and notifications remain functional.
- Ledger remains tamper-evident and AI credit stays with the human owner/reviewer.

IMPORTANT: No real money or real personal data is used. Payments are simulated for the hackathon PoC.
